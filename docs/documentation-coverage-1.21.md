### Other source surfaces

- The sidebar in `site/sidebars.js` exposes the worked example and release history. `site/src/pages/` links directly to the worked change and explains current installation choices. The Docusaurus config assigns the authored guides and catalog separate routes.
- The catalog generator reads the flat `skills/*/SKILL.md` inventory. Build the site to regenerate `site/docs-catalog/`. Edit the owning skill to change its actual instructions. Skill-local READMEs, references and packaged upstream licenses travel with those skills.
- `dist/plugins/`, marketplace manifests and generated harness copies reflect shipping packages. Documentation changes here require no distribution regeneration.
- Historical KBD, OpenSpec and workstream records identify their own revisions and evidence. Preserve those records; the release timeline links representative decisions.
