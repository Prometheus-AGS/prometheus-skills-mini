## Why
The installed Boss forces UAR to embedded SurrealDB and obscures service-operation feedback. Operators need usable administration of existing or bundled services, Compass projects, and gateway models without conflicting with the full Prometheus pack.

## What Changes
- Dedicated UAR, Compass, liter-llm and Services settings with typed preferences, IPC, complete translations and upstream-compatible feature boundaries.
- Discover and explicitly select existing SurrealDB and liter-llm installations; retain embedded UAR and separate service lifecycle ownership.
- Stream action-local progress with durable full logs, cancellation and actionable outcomes.
- Persist Compass project enablement and expose indexing and semantic drift checks.
- Sync the liter-llm fork with upstream, package its provider/model catalogs, administer provider connections/model mappings and configure critic/judge/backup roles.
- Deliver through integration boundaries and native customer-priority release builds; require installed Windows x64 and Apple Silicon acceptance.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- integration-administration: cohesive application administration, service selection and observable operations.
### Modified Capabilities
None. This adds acceptance repairs to uar-delivery-replan P1 and pulls its remote-storage administration forward; remaining runtime phases retain their scope.

## Impact
The Boss settings/routes/preferences/IPC/integration services/UAR launcher; UAR scoped SurrealDB auth; Compass freshness CLI only if custom graph location is unsupported; mini Docker/discovery/review role configuration; liter-llm fork/catalog pin; full-pack shared role resolver when needed. Packaging manifests and release website follow completed builds. No shipped DB migration will be rewritten.
