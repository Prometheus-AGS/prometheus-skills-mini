# Proposal

## Why

The completed Bossfang–UAR implementation exists as 241 uncommitted selected paths across isolated worktrees. Release intake must carry that complete delta and its established compatibility baseline without overwriting primary checkouts or silently importing a backport.

## What Changes

- Produce isolated, scoped UAR, Boss and Bossfang integration checkpoints from the approved finite inventory, including additions, deletions, modes and content identity.
- Preserve UAR/Boss target-only work and use the explicit accepted Bossfang bac04 baseline; disclose its 279 newer commits beyond old main.
- Bind strict approval producers/consumers, UAR harness execution ownership and application-owned MCP configuration as a coordinated candidate.
- Produce a portable source-intake receipt separating new phase deltas from inherited baseline history and retained acceptance.
- Preserve operator-deferred certification and cancelled F6; do not expand external receiver/IdP scope.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `release-source-intake`: Complete, isolated and evidence-bound transfer of a selected cross-repository release source set.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing `execution-bound-authorization`, `job-harness-delegation`, `mcp-resource-authority` and `uar-principal-authority` behavior is preserved, not respecified here.
